## Overview

The tooling and pinning that every later phase and the host-side runtime gate rely on: a rebased branch, the shared constants file that pins the wasm to `PANDOC_PIN`, a way to capture the exact inputs of a real native pandoc run (nothing retains them today), a comparison CLI, and an early, reversible exploration of a versioned, hashable request. Mostly no production Rust.

## Decisions

- The wasm is pinned to `PANDOC_PIN` (3.11), an exact pin beside the `version::gate` floor (D12).
- The request epic's schema may carry `schema_version`/`job_id` fields; the exploration here recommends what goes in, and if it is inconclusive R1 ships `schema_version` and an optional `job_id` and defers the rest.
