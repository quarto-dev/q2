A callout is a box around blocks. Its kind (note, tip, warning, caution, important) is a setting of the box, like its appearance, whether it collapses, its icon and its number: changing one keeps everything in it. Try changing kinds, appearances and collapsing on the callouts below, and making one with Callout in the toolbar (with the caret in it again, the same button takes it away and keeps its contents). Some settings take away the place other settings live: a simple or minimal callout has no header without a title, and a minimal one has no icon. Changing the kind of a numbered one renames its label (#nte- → #tip-) and the references to it. The choices on the left change how the controls work; the address keeps them.
