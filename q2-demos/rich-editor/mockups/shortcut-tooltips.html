Toolbar shortcut tooltips

Each option shows its tooltip under one button. Hover the other buttons to see theirs (after a short delay, then instantly as you move along the toolbar, as macOS does). Undo and redo's shortcuts exist; the others are proposals, following Google Docs: ⇧⌘8 bulleted list, ⇧⌘7 numbered list, ⇧⌘9 quote, ⌥⌘0–⌥⌘4 paragraph and headings. The Markdown hints (*, 1., >, #, each and a space) already work as you type; * is the bullet q2 writes.
