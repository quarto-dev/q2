Figures and callouts are both objects: a box with fields in it (a figure's caption; a callout's title and text). How they look and their controls are settled (figure.html, callouts.html); this compares only how a selection treats a callout, chosen on the left. A figure is selected whole in both: a selection that crosses its edge takes all of it.

Try, in each model

  • Select from the middle of a paragraph into the middle of a caption, or of a callout's text, with Shift and the arrows, a Shift-click or a drag. Then back out again.
  • Select from inside a callout's text out past its end.
  • With that selected, copy (⌘C) and paste (⌘V) it somewhere else: into a line, or into an empty paragraph. Cut (⌘X) or Backspace it.
  • Drag what's selected somewhere else.
  • Copy a selection that takes part of a callout and paste it into another app: a plain text editor gets only the text selected; a document (Google Docs, say) gets the callout as a box with only that much in it.
  • Click an image to select its figure.
